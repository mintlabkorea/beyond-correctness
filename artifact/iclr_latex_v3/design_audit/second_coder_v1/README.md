# Second AI coder: all 25 comparisons

Primary v1 is unchanged. A fresh-context second AI agent independently declared the tested semantic use and protected set, froze those declarations, coded all 25 comparisons, and froze its codes before comparison. Primary scopes/codes and extracted outcome artifacts were not supplied. Full source PDFs can expose results incidentally; outcomes were not extracted or used in the independent coding.

The primary concern is borne out: Preservation is sensitive to independently declared protection and operational interpretation. Admissible comparisons change from 4/25 to 5/25; studies with at least one admissible reference change from 3/9 to 4/9. TabLLM values-only changes from No to Yes under a scope protecting ordered value slots without protecting name-prefix scaffolding. Seven other comparisons change from No to Unclear; FeatLLM changes from Unclear to No when type/category metadata is protected. No outcome-based adjudication or secondary outcome join was performed.
