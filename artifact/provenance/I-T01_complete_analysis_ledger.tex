\section{Complete analysis provenance audit}
\label{sec:app-registry}

The table below provides the complete analysis-provenance ledger for the
supplement, including when each analysis was defined, its outcome under the
stated decision criterion, the resulting interpretation, and its location.
It is not treated as a single family of confirmatory hypotheses, and no
table-wide FWER correction is introduced.

An outcome of \emph{criterion not met} means that the stated decision
criterion was not satisfied, not that the computation failed.
\emph{No eligible case} indicates that the corresponding outcome-blind audit
found no case meeting its eligibility rule.
