\subsection{Joint measurement--correspondence surface}

The main factorial contrast uses the four corners of the $M\times C$ design.
Figure~\ref{fig:app-mc-surface} evaluates the full $5\times5$ surface.  The
stored quantity is normalized MSE, so error generally rises with either
corruption dose.  The prespecified moderate rectangle $I[0,4;0,4]$ is positive
and separated in all six family--tree strata, and the average interior
adjacent rectangle is also positive.  The interaction is therefore not solely
an extreme-corner or floor artifact.
