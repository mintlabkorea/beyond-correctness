The five-point source-integrity ladder extends to TabPFN, with all three
family curves declining and crossing sign in the final interval.  The
triggered source-mass ladder does not identify a mass-proportional mechanism:
all six slope intervals include zero, and the prespecified classification is
\emph{mass versus collision unresolved}.  Independently, source-only endpoint
AUROC fails to predict the ten-endpoint real-data interaction
($\rho=-.030$, exact $p=.541$).  These outcomes remain together in
Table~\ref{tab:app-composition-integrity-extensions} so that the controlled
integrity effect is not generalized to the real endpoint panel.
