The pre-existing XGB ten-endpoint factorial contains all four conditions and hence
both correspondence simple effects.  On the polarity-invariant protocol
score, $f(A_{01})-f(A_{00})$ (measurement knowledge not supplied) was
$-.0116~[-.0385,+.0152]$ with 4/10 positive endpoints, whereas
$f(A_{11})-f(A_{10})$ (measurement knowledge supplied as intended) was
$+.0195~[+.0031,+.0360]$ with 9/10 positive.  Their difference, the
ten-endpoint $I^*_{MC}$ point estimate, is $+.0311$ at the displayed
four-decimal precision (unrounded: $+.031151$, conventionally reported as
$+.0312$).  On raw AUROC the two
simple effects were $-.0277~[-.0669,+.0116]$ and
$+.0195~[+.0031,+.0360]$, respectively.  Table~\ref{tab:app-factorial-c-simple-effects}
reports every raw condition mean and both raw and folded point contrasts for every
endpoint.  This is an exploratory analysis of completed cells, not a
post-result choice between the two simple effects.

A post-result interaction-level sensitivity gives a pooled raw-AUROC
interaction of $+.0472$ [$+.0178,+.0861$], compared with $+.0312$
[$+.0082,+.0582$] under the prespecified polarity-invariant score. Their paired
difference is $+.0161$ [$+.0003,+.0372$], so condition-wise folding does not
create the positive pooled interaction; if anything, it reduces its magnitude.
This result concerns the interaction level only. The labeled-target-label
corruption ladder still does not show the corresponding attenuation on raw
AUROC.
