Table~\ref{tab:app-controlled-factorial} gives every family-specific
contrast for measurement knowledge, correspondence knowledge, and $I_{MC}$. The main tree results
separate at both labeled-target sizes; TabPFN reproduces all three effects at
$K=32$; and the Frozen-before-execution MLP extension also separates in every family and
budget.  These replications establish the observed composition contrast
across the tested predictive models, not equality of effect sizes or a
universal routing mechanism.
