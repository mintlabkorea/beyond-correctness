\subsection{Controlled calibration and transfer boundaries}
\label{sec:app-controlled-boundaries}

Table~\ref{tab:app-layer-corruption-dose} reports the original corruption-dose
experiment for each kind of semantic knowledge, rather than the later mismatch
experiment for measurement knowledge.  Corrupting measurement knowledge,
correspondence knowledge, or relation knowledge increases error
monotonically in every prespecified XGBoost family stratum.  This graded loss in
performance does not establish predictive utility relative to a valid reference.

The exchangeable correspondence control is not uniformly clean.  In the
original two-draw comparison, the pairwise family gives $+.105$ and $+.108$
for XGB and HistGB and fails the prespecified $\pm.05$ equivalence margin.
Exhaustive enumeration of all 81 admissible correspondence corruptions places
those draws near a two-sided five-percent tail and confirms a larger
intended-versus-corruption-average
effect in every family and tree
(Table~\ref{tab:app-correspondence-exchangeable-calibration}).  Enumeration
calibrates the excursion; it does not retroactively convert the failed margin
into a pass.
