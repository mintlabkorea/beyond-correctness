\paragraph{Conventional cluster-level resolution.}
To separate decision-rule-specific thresholds from a familiar power calculation,
we also averaged paired gains within each independent endpoint or basin and
treated the observed between-unit standard deviation as a planning value.  We
then solved the noncentral-$t$ power equation for the minimum positive mean
giving 80\% power under a two-sided one-sample test at $\alpha=.05$.
Table~\ref{tab:app-real-utility-resolution} reports the results.  The
calculation is post-hoc and descriptive, and is especially
uncertain when the planning standard deviation comes from three endpoints.
