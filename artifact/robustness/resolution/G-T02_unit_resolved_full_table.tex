\subsection{Unit-resolved heterogeneity and extended CAMELS scope}

Table~\ref{tab:app-unit-attribution} reports endpoint- and basin-level means
behind the aggregate attribution tables.  These rows are descriptive, not
independent seed-level tests.  Content-sensitivity effects for relation knowledge have a consistent sign
across all HRS endpoints and the original 12 CAMELS basins, whereas their
predictive utilities fluctuate around zero.  Predictive utility for correspondence knowledge is heterogeneous
across survey endpoints even when intended-versus-wrong content sensitivity separates.
The 120-basin CAMELS extension is an additional analysis fixed before execution:
content sensitivity replicates and the utility remains unresolved on its own metric
scale (Table~\ref{tab:app-relation-full}).
