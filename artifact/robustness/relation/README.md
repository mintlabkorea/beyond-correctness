# robustness/relation

`category_index.csv` is the full file index; paths are relative to the package root. Scientific records retain their original `experiments/` and `scripts/` locations. See `reproducibility/table_figure_map.csv` and `ARTIFACT_STATUS.md`.

Computed-value relation interfaces (constituent removal, noisy proxies, reconstructibility) and executable-direction interfaces (documented/reversed/random/unconstrained and capacity tests) remain separate experiment directories. Reconstruction R2 cannot distinguish a value from its negation. The real-data probes retain the failed manipulation criteria and do not establish a general real-data mechanism.

D.2 is reconstructed by reproducibility/audit_aggregate_records.py from CELL_DOSE_V1.csv. within_dose_recomputed.csv has 48 per-setting coefficients; within_dose_dose_means.csv averages eight settings at each of six nonzero doses. Its extrema .16466165 and .32857143 reproduce the printed .165–.329. historical_within_dose_table.tex is a recovered historical table defining that aggregation; within_dose_provenance.json records its origin/hash. within_dose_setting_means.csv averages across doses instead and is only an alternative diagnostic, not the paper estimand. The earlier packaging report compared the wrong averaging axis; no paper value was changed for D.2.
