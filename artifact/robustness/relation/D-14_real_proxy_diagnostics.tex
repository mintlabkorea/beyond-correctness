For CAMELS-120, the initial zero-noise proxy $R^2$ was $.95775$, but the proxy
frame and downstream reference differed by two zero-padding columns.  With
\texttt{colsample\_bytree}=.8, those otherwise uninformative columns change
feature-sampling probabilities.  Adding the same two columns to the proxy
frame reduces the width-aligned estimate to $.94025$, below the frozen $.95$
gate.  More importantly, as reconstructibility fell, the reference improved
rather than deteriorated: its $\sigma=4$ minus $\sigma=0$ change is $-.00458$
versus $-.00153$ for the intended condition, and
$\rho(1-R^2,\text{reference error})=-.343$ [$-.693,+.007$].  The required
reference-only weakening was therefore not created.  This is an uninformative
test whose conditions were not established, not a falsification.  The audit
is another instance of the admissible-reference requirement: width and
learner mechanics must be matched, as in the earlier width-matched examination
reference.

For NH$\rightarrow$KN, the zero-noise reconstruction $R^2$ is $.80945$ and
mean utility is $+.000016$ normalized-RMSE.  The reference deteriorates by
$+.00197$, but the intended condition also deteriorates by $+.00110$;
$\rho(1-R^2,\text{reference error})=+.361$ [$+.179,+.529$].  The relationship
is therefore not established.  Target-only linear reconstruction gives
$R^2=1.000$, source-only linear transfer gives $.619$, and adding target
support raises it to $.885$; target-only full-frame XGB gives $.874$, whereas
the frozen source-plus-support XGB reconstructor gives $.809$.  Cross-table
reconstruction consequently reflects schema shift and learner capacity as
well as algebraic redundancy.

A post-hoc orthogonal decomposition is descriptive only.  Writing
$m=(\mathrm{SBP}+\mathrm{DBP})/2$ and
$d=\mathrm{SBP}-\mathrm{DBP}$, independent SBP/DBP noise changes reference
and intended error by $+.00197$ and $+.00110$.  Difference-only perturbation,
which preserves $m$, changes them by only $+.00021$ and $+.00005$; common-mode
perturbation, which preserves $d$, changes them by $+.00184$ and $+.00148$.
Thus much of the original intended degradation is consistent with losing
base-frame information orthogonal to the supplied relation value.  Under
difference-only perturbation the intended condition is nearly flat, but the
reference is also nearly flat, suggesting little useful contribution from
this tested relation in these endpoints.  This diagnostic does not validate
or falsify the proxy in real data, and low reconstructibility does not by
itself guarantee predictive utility.
