Comparison: LIFT C01-B — Correct-Names II vs Shuffled-Names II.
Record created: 2026-09-24, during artifact finalization. This is the record-creation date, not an asserted historical adjudication date.

## Independent initial coding

Coder A: Unclear.

Reason: The paper did not provide a rendered Shuffled-Names II example, so it was initially unclear whether sentence structure or wording changed in addition to the feature-name assignment.

Coder B: Supported.

Reason: The paper and supplement describe the shuffled-name condition as using the same prompt construction with randomly shuffled feature names.

## Source re-check supplied for finalization

The construction-source check records that the shuffled-name conditions use the same prompts with feature names randomly shuffled while the feature values remain unchanged.

Relevant source: LIFT Sec. 4.1 / Table 9 and Appendix D.2.1. Original checks are retained in claim_coding_coder_a.csv (construction_source_checked) and claim_coding_coder_b.csv (construction_evidence), row C01-B.

## Final adjudication

Supported.

Rationale: The tested semantic content—the feature-name/value association—is altered, while the predictive values, prompt-format family, model, and learning procedure are held fixed according to the published construction description.
