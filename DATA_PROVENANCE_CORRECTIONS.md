# Data provenance corrections

## CAMELS-GB
The hydrological experiments used CAMELS-GB v2
(Coxon et al., 2025; DOI 10.5285/9a46d428-958f-4ac1-86eb-94eee70c0955), not the 2020 original release as
described in the frozen artifact/DATA_ACCESS.md.

## HRS source sample size
The public manuscript previously described the HRS relation source
sample as capped at 4,096 rows. The executed relation experiments used
all eligible pooled NHANES/KNHANES source rows, with endpoint-specific
sample sizes ranging from 58,737 to 126,500.

These corrections affect provenance and sample-description text only;
the reported experimental results were generated from the recorded
inputs and are unchanged.
